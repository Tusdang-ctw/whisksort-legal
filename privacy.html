Privacy Policy

Effective 17 November 2026

This policy explains what information the game Whisk & Sort (the "game") collects, why, and what you can do about it. The game is made and published by Dang Viet Tu, an individual developer ("I", "me"). You can reach me at ashdang263@gmail.com.

The short version

  • You can play without an account. Your progress then stays on your device.
  • If you choose to sign in, your game progress is saved online so you can restore it on another device.
  • There are no ads and no advertising trackers. The game uses Google Analytics for Firebase to learn how the game is played, without linking it to your account. It is on by default, and you can turn it off with the Share usage data switch in Settings.
  • I never sell your information or share it for advertising.

Information the game uses

On your device. Your levels, coins, gems, café decorations, staff and settings (music, sound, haptics, language) are stored on your device. This information does not leave your device unless you sign in.

If you sign in with Apple or Google. Signing in is optional. When you sign in, the game receives an account identifier from Apple or Google and, depending on what you allow, your email address and name. With Sign in with Apple you can choose to hide your email address. Your game progress is then saved to the cloud and linked to that account.

Purchases. In-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple. I never see your payment card or billing details. The game receives a record of what was bought (the product and a transaction identifier) so it can deliver the item and, if you are signed in, keep a purchase history with your saved progress.

How the game is played (analytics). The game uses Google Analytics for Firebase to record how it is used: for example which levels you play, which screens you open, how long a session lasts and which items are bought. This comes with basic device information (model, iOS version, language, and an approximate country or region worked out from your connection) and a random identifier for this installation of the game. It is not linked to your name, email or game account, the game does not collect your device's advertising identifier, and it is not used for advertising or to track you across other apps and websites. Analytics is on by default. You can turn it off at any time with the Share usage data switch in the game's Settings.

Protecting the game (App Check). To stop modified copies of the game from using the online services, the game uses Firebase App Check with Apple's App Attest. Your device confirms to Apple and Firebase that it is running the genuine game. This check carries no personal information about you and is not used for analytics or advertising.

Reminders. If you allow them, the game schedules a daily reminder on your device. Reminders are created and shown entirely on your device, and no information is sent anywhere for them. You can turn them off in iOS Settings › Notifications.

Why it is used

  • To run the game and remember your progress.
  • To restore your progress when you sign in on another device.
  • To deliver items you buy and help with purchase questions.
  • To answer you when you contact support.
  • To understand which levels and features players enjoy, find problems and improve the game (analytics).

Your information is not used for advertising, profiling or tracking across other apps and websites.

Who processes it

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC) stores sign-in accounts and cloud saves, and runs App Check. Its servers may be outside your country. See Firebase privacy information.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase (Google LLC) processes the analytics described above. Advertising features and Google signals are switched off. See Firebase privacy information.
  • Apple processes purchases and Sign in with Apple. See Apple's privacy policy.
  • Google processes Sign in with Google. See Google's privacy policy.

I do not share your information with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

How long it is kept

Information on your device is removed when you delete the game. Cloud saves and account information are kept until you delete your account, after which they are deleted from the cloud within 30 days. Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months and then deleted automatically. Support emails are kept only as long as needed to help you.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: in the game, open Settings, then your account, and choose Delete account. You can also email me and I will delete it for you.
  • Access or correct: email me to ask for a copy of the information linked to your account or to correct it.
  • Turn off analytics: in the game, open Settings and switch off Share usage data. The game keeps working exactly the same.
  • Stop syncing: sign out at any time. The game keeps working with progress stored on your device.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or Vietnam's Decree 13/2023 on personal data protection), you may have further rights, such as objecting to processing or complaining to a data protection authority. Email me and I will answer within 30 days.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not need any personal information to play. Signing in is optional. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has signed in and shared personal information, contact me and I will delete it.

Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections and stored with Firebase's security controls. No system is perfectly secure, but I take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Important changes will also be shown in the game.
