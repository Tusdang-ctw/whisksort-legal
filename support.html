A purchase did not arrive

Close and reopen the game while connected to the internet. If the item is still missing, email me with the date of the purchase. Apple's receipt email shows it. For refunds, use reportaproblem.apple.com.

I got a new phone. How do I keep my progress?

Sign in with Apple or Google from Settings on your old device first, then sign in with the same account on the new one. Without signing in, progress is stored only on the device it was played on.

How do I change the language?

Open Settings in the game and choose English or Tiếng Việt.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings, then your account, and choose Delete account. Or email me and I will do it for you. See the Privacy Policy for details.
